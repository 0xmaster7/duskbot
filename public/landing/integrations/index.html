dusk bot/integrations

integrations

connect an app once and a bot can work inside it. each page lists what it can really do there, what it asks you first, and routines to copy.

apps

  • excelUse the Microsoft Excel connection in Dusk Bot, or drop an .xlsx file into chat. Check ranges, review tables and prepare workbook reports.
  • githubGive a bot in Dusk Bot a GitHub repo to follow. Get issue triage, pull request context and release notes, with clear limits on what it can change.
  • gmailConnect Gmail to a bot in Dusk Bot running on Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini. What it can really do in your inbox, what it asks first, and three routines to copy.
  • google calendarConnect Google Calendar to Dusk Bot for availability checks, focus-time proposals and recurring meeting reviews. Three routines with clear edit boundaries.
  • google sheetsConnect Google Sheets to Dusk Bot for row checks, table updates and charts. Copy focused routines that name their input range and write boundary.
  • notionGive a bot in Dusk Bot a focused Notion job: check incomplete records, collect decisions or flag stale pages. Real actions, routines and edit limits.
  • outlookUse Dusk Bot with Outlook to prepare meeting context, find promised replies and review folders. See the actual actions and where approvals apply.
  • slackRead Slack threads with a bot in Dusk Bot, collect decisions and prepare replies. Three focused routines, plus controls for reading and posting.
