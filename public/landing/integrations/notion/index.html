dusk bot/integrations

a notion bot that keeps the next step easy to find

connect notion and a bot in dusk bot can find pages, read their text and update the records you tell it to maintain. it works with the ai you choose for that bot. begin with a report of missing information in chat. page edits and database updates need clear boundaries because the sending rule does not make them ask first.

by the dusk bot team · checked 12 november 2026 · how we made this

what a bot can do in Notion

a useful notion bot knows which page is the source and which page may receive new text. these actions let it read a workspace, inspect a record and add a bounded update without treating every page as a blank canvas.

actiondusk bot treats it aswhat you would use it for
Search Notion pages and databasesreadfind the project home by its title
Get page markdownreadread a document as text before suggesting a revision
Fetch All Notion Block Contentsreadcollect page blocks for a content review
Fetch Databasereadinspect the database before interpreting its records
Fetch database rowreadcheck the fields on a particular project record
Fetch commentsreadinclude review comments when assessing an unfinished page
Append text blocks (paragraphs, headings, lists)changeadd a dated decision note beneath an agreed heading
Update Database Row (Page)changeupdate a specific record after you define the field rules

composio, the connection service dusk bot uses for apps, lists 60 Notion actions (catalog version 20261101_00, read 12 november 20265). dusk bot treats 24 of them as reads, 2 as sends and the other 34 as changes. an action counts as a read only when its name starts with a read word like get, list or search, so a bot you allow only to read can never call the rest. a send is anything that reaches a person outside the app or moves money, like create comment: it waits for your Allow unless you've given that bot a daily amount it may send alone.

connect Notion

  1. open Connected apps in the sidebar.
  2. pick Notion and sign in on Notion's own page, so your password goes to them and never to a bot.
  3. choose which bots may use it, and whether each may only read or may also change things. your bookkeeper doesn't need your Notion.
  4. ask in plain words. reading and drafting happen freely; sending asks you first.

connect notion through Connected apps and give access to the bot responsible for documentation. notion handles the sign-in. share a page reference in the chat and identify the destination separately. start by asking the bot to read and describe what it found before enabling any upkeep.

routines to copy

pick one source of truth for each job. the routines below keep incomplete fields, decision capture and document age as separate questions.

record completeness

each monday at 2, inspect the notion project records i linked in this chat. list any missing owner, next step or target date. do not fill gaps by guessing and do not edit the records. group your findings by project.

mondays at 14:00 · an incomplete-record checklist

decision note draft

every thursday at 4, read the acme meeting notes page in notion. collect decisions explicitly agreed this week and the reason stated for each. prepare an addition for the decision log here. ask me before you change anything in notion.

thursdays at 16:00 · a proposed decision-log addition

old instructions check

on the first monday of each month at 10, read the notion onboarding pages linked here. flag dated instructions, unresolved comments and references that need an owner to check them. explain what needs verification; do not rewrite or archive pages.

first monday of the month at 10:00 · a maintenance list with reasons to inspect each page

what it asks you first

notion page edits and record updates are app changes. they are not promised an Allow card by the sending rule. a read-only bot cannot make those changes, and an action restriction can narrow a writer to only the tools it needs.

if you want a writer to propose its work first, include "ask me before you change anything" in the routine. comments are posts, so sending controls apply to posting them; keep comments disabled if the job is only to review privately.

what it cannot do

  • rewrite, move or archive a notion page under read-only access. a private summary of that page is a different job and does not require those changes.
  • turn an absent owner or date into a fact. the records and notes have to supply that information; the routine should flag a gap for you rather than quietly assigning someone.
  • maintain a workspace on schedule while the local machine is asleep. cloud hosting keeps the bot available; the desktop app needs to stay open on an awake computer.

separate the evidence from the destination

give the bot the meeting notes as a source and the decision log as a possible destination. explain which headings it may append beneath. if delivery work is tracked in linear, let the notion page link to it instead of asking the bot to infer progress from prose.

choose the place where notes belong

a connected notion workspace and an obsidian folder are different setups. the former uses app actions; the latter is a set of files on your computer. for a broader planning workflow, the product manager page connects these smaller jobs to a working week.

questions

can i use claude with notion pages?

yes. choose claude or another supported ai for a bot in dusk bot, then connect notion and grant the actions needed for the page job. the bot keeps its own chats and memory.

can it maintain my wiki without changing everything?

start with a reader that reports pages needing attention. for writing, name the destination and allowed edit, and tell the routine to ask before making any change.

does a bot remember the way we write project notes?

you can tell it your rules and it can keep memory notes. read and edit those notes in the bot's Memory settings, rather than relying on an unwritten convention.
