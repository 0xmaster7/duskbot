poke vs dusk bot: where you text your assistant

poke is a personal assistant you text like a contact, through services including apple messages, telegram, whatsapp and rcs.12 dusk bot has its own desktop and paired phone interface, where you text separate bots with different jobs. poke runs as an always-on cloud agent; dusk bots can live on your computer, your server or dusk cloud.37 choose first by where you want the conversation and whether you want a single assistant or named roles.

by the dusk bot team · checked 12 november 2026 · how we made this

the short version

pick poke if

  • you want to text an assistant through apple messages, telegram, whatsapp or rcs
  • you want cloud operation and ai included in the same service plan

pick dusk bot if

  • you want different bots for different responsibilities, with their own memory
  • you want control over where the bots live and which ai each one uses

a phone assistant should fit the way you check in

if you want the conversation in an existing messaging app, poke directly supports that pattern.2 there is no reason to add a separate bot workspace merely to ask a small question. the assistants you text comparison looks at that preference across products.

dusk bot's phone interface is different. you pair it once with a computer or dusk cloud, then text your bots there. today pairing opens a browser version you can add to the home screen. that version sends no notifications; the store apps are still on their way.6 if you rely on a notification to notice work, take that limitation seriously.

the closed laptop changes the dusk choice

poke is described as an always-on cloud agent.3 a dusk bot on your laptop still depends on the app running and the machine being on. pairing a phone to that laptop does not move the bot to the phone.6

with dusk cloud, the bots run on a computer we host and your phone works with the laptop off. the cloud computer is your own machine, not shared, and ai usage remains a separate provider cost.79 the personal agent guide is a useful next step if you want to choose where the bot should live before assigning more work.

split responsibilities only when it helps

in dusk bot, you might keep one bot for planning a writing project and another for reviewing attached drafts. each has its own memory and ai choice.8 give the planner a routine such as: "every monday at 9, ask me which writing commitment matters most this week. keep the question in this chat."

that arrangement adds roles to manage. if you simply want one contact to remind you about things, poke's documented reminder and scheduling features may be a closer fit.1 poke alternatives covers other choices without assuming more bots are always useful.

for dusk bots connected to apps, keep early trials read-only. sends ask first unless you set a daily allowance, but other changes inside an app can go ahead without asking. one exception: a bot on Full access with no sending rule of its own may send up to 25 a day without asking, so give full-access bots Ask every time under sending.12 request "ask me before you change anything" if you later grant write access.11

how we checked

poke's pages don't say which models it uses or how it asks before acting; that doesn't mean it has no safeguards.

questions

can poke message me first?

yes. as of 12 november 2026, poke's homepage describes proactive messages, reminders and scheduled tasks. it lists background automations on pro.1

how do i run an agent from my phone when my laptop is closed?

poke already runs in the cloud.3 for dusk bot, pair the phone to dusk cloud rather than a sleeping laptop. a phone paired to your computer needs that computer on and running dusk bot.67

is poke joining cognition?

yes. cognition announced it was welcoming the interaction company on 23 july 2026 and said poke users could continue using the product as before. on 12 november 2026 poke's homepage still showed 'poke is joining cognition'.13
