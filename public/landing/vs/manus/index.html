manus vs dusk bot: a task service or bots you keep

manus is a general-purpose agent that works in cloud computers and produces things such as research, websites and slides.1 dusk bot is an app for keeping bots with lasting jobs, chats and memory. manus packages models and tasks into credit-based plans; dusk separates the app or hosted computer from your ai provider's bill.25 choose by the work you want to hand over and how you prefer to budget, rather than an unsupported cost-per-task claim.

by the dusk bot team · checked 12 november 2026 · how we made this

the short version

pick manus if

  • you want a cloud task service that produces research, slides or websites
  • you want the agent's models packaged into a credit-based plan rather than arranging separate ai billing

pick dusk bot if

  • you want a small set of bots that keep their roles and memory between jobs
  • you want to choose an ai per bot and decide separately whether to pay for hosting

ask whether you are buying a task or keeping a role

manus describes an agent that can produce research, websites, slides, images and video.1 if you want a particular deliverable from a hosted service, that is a direct place to start. its telegram integration also describes recurring tasks delivered back into chat.3

in dusk bot you keep a bot with a job and memory you can edit. changing the bot's ai keeps its chats, files and memory.4 that can suit an ongoing reviewer who follows how your plans develop. that's a different way of working, not a promise of a better report. the market research use case helps narrow the brief before choosing a service.

manus said on 1 september 2026 that it has resumed independent operations.12

the price calculation has an unknown

as of 12 november 2026, manus pro lists $20 a month and 4,000 monthly credits. max lists $200 and 40,000 monthly credits, with pay-as-you-go after credits.2 the free tier's 300 daily credits are a daily allowance, not the same kind of monthly pool.

we cannot turn those figures into a reliable number of research reports. nothing on manus's pricing page tells you how many credits your task will use. write down the work you expect, inspect actual usage on a trial and then compare the plan.

for dusk bot, the bill depends on where the bots live and which ai you choose. your own computer is free for personal use; cloud plus is $20 a month and pro is $50. ai usage stays with your provider.58 work use needs a work license, which cloud pro includes for one person.811 ai agent costs covers those separate budget lines.

test with a brief that exposes missing evidence

give the service a small set of documents and ask: "compare the proposals against these requirements. show which claims are supported by the files and mark missing evidence. do not treat a missing answer as a yes." check a few claims against the originals before accepting the conclusion.

with a dusk bot, keep that first review within attached files. you can inspect and edit the bot's saved notes under Memory and turn off Let this bot use memory if you do not want it drawing on stored notes.10 memory is not a source citation. if you want other ways to package the job, manus alternatives is the broader comparison.

how we checked

we build dusk bot. we read manus's own product and pricing pages on 12 november 2026, compared them with dusk bot's docs, and did not run identical tasks through both. manus credit consumption for a matching job was not measured, so there is no claim that either product is cheaper per report. the table also does not rank their research accuracy or finished documents.

questions

how many free credits does manus ai give?

its pricing page lists 300 credits a day on free as of 12 november 2026, with manus 2.0 lite, 3 concurrent tasks and 2 automations. that allowance does not establish how many of your tasks will finish.2

what should i compare before switching from manus?

compare the task you need, the allowed usage and where the work lives. if recurring research is the reason for switching, test the same narrow brief and inspect sources as well as usage. we haven't run the same task through both, so we can't say which is better or cheaper.

can i message manus from telegram?

yes. the telegram launch post documents linking the account and using manus agents there, including recurring summaries.3 dusk bot uses its own desktop and paired phone interface; its store phone apps are still on the way.7
