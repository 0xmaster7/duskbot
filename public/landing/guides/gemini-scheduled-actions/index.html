gemini scheduled actions: what to expect at delivery

as of 12 november 2026, gemini apps supports recurring actions with up to ten active schedules. keep activity must be on, and access is rolling out gradually to personal google accounts and qualifying work or school accounts. gemini prepares content in the hour before delivery. that makes it a fit for recurring reading, but it is separate from gemini cli or antigravity automation and their terms.

by the dusk bot team · checked 12 november 2026 · how we made this

check account access before buying a plan

as of 12 november 2026, google's help page describes schedules for personal google accounts and work or school accounts with a qualifying workspace edition. Keep Activity must be on. google says the feature is gradually becoming available to personal accounts, so it may not appear for everyone yet.1

the page does not state a paid google ai plan requirement. avoid assuming a missing feature means you must upgrade. first check the account, activity setting and current eligibility page. a work account is not interchangeable with your personal one simply because both open gemini.1

if you cannot find schedules, do not keep creating variations of the same chat instruction and assume one will enable rollout. confirm availability first, then save one useful recurring action.

delivery time is different from the time the reading happens

as of 12 november 2026, gemini's help page says recurring actions can be part of daily, weekly and monthly routines. it prepares content in the background within the hour before delivery. notifications appear in the web app sidebar and on mobile.1

that preparation window matters for your choice of job. a morning reading digest can still be useful when the source was checked shortly before delivery. if you need news from the last few minutes, a schedule isn't the right tool. do not treat a delivery time as proof that every source was read at that second.

include the period you care about in the prompt. 'the past week' is clearer than 'recently'. ask for source dates and for gaps to be named. a short digest with an honest missing source is easier to use than a confident summary with no time boundary.

three recurring actions worth shaping first

try a prompt in chat before asking for repetition. change these to fit your own topics.

  • a daily topic brief: 'each morning, give me three developments in urban transport policy. include the source date and link for each. distinguish proposals from rules already in force.'
  • a weekly research question: 'every friday, review new public material about battery recycling. explain one disagreement between sources and what evidence would resolve it.'
  • a monthly reading plan: 'on the first of the month, suggest a small reading list for learning architectural history. tell me the level and why each item belongs.'

choose one purpose and a short output. if a brief feels repetitive, narrow its topic or reduce its frequency before adding more schedules. ten active slots are a ceiling to work within, not a target you need to fill.

gemini apps schedules are separate from the cli

as of 12 november 2026, google's published transition says gemini cli stopped serving google ai pro, ultra and free individual accounts on 18 june 2026, with individuals moving to antigravity cli. an article about gemini app schedules does not change that cli transition.2

antigravity's faq says headless agy -p is intended for direct scripting and ci, and that wrapping agy inside third-party coding agents or applications is not supported under consumer plans. the additional terms call third-party software access a breach that may lead to suspension or termination.34

that's google's own wording; read its current terms for the way you plan to sign in. check your provider's current terms for your access method. the what each ai company says about sign-ins follows those changes without treating gemini apps, gemini cli and antigravity as one product.

when another routine setup fits better

stay with gemini schedules if a regular brief in that app is the result you want and your account has access. compare chatgpt tasks if plan-based active limits or supported events are central to your choice. the scheduler comparison puts those differences together.

a dusk routine belongs to a bot with a name, job, chats and its own memory. make it by telling the bot what should repeat, then review the schedule card. local dusk routines stop during sleep or when the app is closed; dusk cloud runs them on time.56

that is a separate place for a recurring job, not a way to bypass google's sign-in rules. choose the ai connection only after reading the applicable terms.

as of 12 november 2026, the schedules help page does not state a paid google ai plan requirement. it lists personal accounts and qualifying workspace accounts, requires keep activity, and notes gradual rollout. check your account's current eligibility first.1

how many gemini scheduled actions can i have?

as of 12 november 2026, google lists up to ten active schedules at a time. use those slots for distinct jobs you want to keep, rather than several versions of the same brief.1

does a scheduled digest use information from the delivery second?

do not assume that. as of 12 november 2026, google says gemini prepares content within the hour before delivery. write a prompt that asks for source dates if freshness matters.1
