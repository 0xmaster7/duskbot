claude cowork pricing: what are you paying for?

claude cowork has no separate add-on price: anthropic includes it in paid claude plans. as of 12 november 2026, pro is $20 a month with monthly billing, max is $100 and ultra is $200, before tax. free does not include cowork. anthropic now describes cowork and chat as one claude, so you may see the cowork name less often.123

cowork is part of the claude plan

anthropic lists cowork on pro, max, ultra, team and enterprise. the consumer prices in the table are from the claude pricing page, checked on 12 november 2026. these are us dollar prices and exclude tax. team is $20 per seat a month with annual billing or $25 with monthly billing; ask anthropic for enterprise pricing.12

pro's annual option is shown as $17 a month with $200 paid up front. that is an annual commitment, not a $17 month-to-month checkout. max and ultra show monthly prices only. check the checkout and provider terms before paying.

the rename does not create a second product bill

anthropic's product page now says cowork and chat are one claude. when work calls for files, claude brings in cowork automatically. it's the same feature with a new name, not something extra to buy.3

older articles may discuss a distinct cowork tab or use the old name throughout. when comparing a tutorial with your account, check its date and the present feature name. the windows and linux guide covers current platform support so you do not choose a plan based on an outdated mac-only description.

the larger plan is about more than the sticker price

claude's pricing page lists max at $100 and ultra at $200. it no longer lists a $200 max 20x tier under that name. max is described as 5x pro usage; ultra as 10x pro weekly usage, with no five-hour limit for verified accounts. do not turn those descriptions into a promise of unlimited work.2

before upgrading, write down the task you cannot complete on the smaller plan. a higher allowance will not fix a brief that is missing the source files or a connection that cannot perform the action you need. choose for the actual constraint rather than the largest number on the page.

scheduled work still consumes usage

cowork's own scheduled tasks are on all paid plans and run remotely, so they keep going while your computer sleeps.6

claude code is a separate product: its cloud scheduled tasks, previously called routines, count against plan usage. they are available on pro, max, ultra, team and enterprise, with a minimum cloud interval of one hour. they run on anthropic-managed infrastructure, so a closed laptop does not stop them.4

if your buying decision is mainly about recurring work, read claude code scheduled tasks and distinguish code tasks from cowork tasks before paying. a feature being included does not mean every frequency or workflow is available without limits.

compare the costs of the workflow you want

list the ai plan, any separately purchased service, and where the work must run. do not count a desktop app price as if it also pays the ai provider. in dusk bot, ai usage is billed by your ai provider on your plan with them and is never on the dusk bill.5

the cowork alternative guide helps compare that different arrangement. dusk bot is free for personal use on your own computer; work use needs a work license. hosted dusk cloud has its own price. compare the parts you will actually need, including licensing, rather than calling the whole arrangement free because one part is.5
