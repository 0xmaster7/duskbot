a personal ai agent that fits your actual day

choose a personal ai agent around a recurring need, not a promise that it will manage your life. decide where you want to talk to it, what it may read or change, and whether it must work with your laptop off. dusk bot fits people who want named bots with separate jobs and memory. a texting service, a cloud task agent or a workflow builder may fit a different need better.

by the dusk bot team · checked 12 november 2026 · how we made this

start with the last thing you wished you had help with

write down an actual task from this week. perhaps you wanted help comparing two documents, preparing questions before an appointment, or keeping track of a small project. choose something with material you can supply and an answer you can inspect.

then ask what would make an agent useful: remembering your preferences, repeating the check, reaching an app, or doing work while you are away. if all you need is a single answer, a longer-running setup may add work you do not need.

use the same task when trying different products. do not judge one on a polished demonstration and another on the messiest job in your life. ask what it missed and how much context you had to provide.

pick the conversation you will actually return to

a phone-first habit is a real reason to choose a tool. poke documents support for apple messages, telegram, whatsapp and rcs. if an existing messaging channel is the deciding factor, put it on your shortlist.3

dusk bot is a desktop app with phone pairing. until its store apps arrive, pairing opens it in your phone browser and you add it to the home screen. that browser version sends no notifications. it is a meaningful limit if you expect an assistant to alert you while you are elsewhere.6

a phone paired to your own computer also needs that computer on and running dusk bot. paired to dusk cloud, it works with your computer off. try phone access in the conditions you expect to use it, rather than only beside an awake laptop.

check memory and access before adding your life

in dusk bot, each bot has its own memory. About me in Settings shares your name, time zone and writing preferences with every bot. you can inspect and edit a bot's notes in its Memory settings, or turn off Let this bot use memory; the files stay on disk.7

start by giving it a harmless preference and seeing whether that helps a later task. memory is useful when it holds information you want reused. it is less useful when an old assumption keeps getting carried into new work.

look at access separately. dusk lets you connect apps per bot, with read-only access where appropriate. sends ask first unless you set a daily amount (or the bot is on Full access with no sending rule of its own, which lets it send up to 25 a day), but other app changes are not documented as automatically asking. start read only, or explicitly say "ask me before you change anything."8

local storage also does not mean your chosen ai runs locally. what you say goes to your ai provider from the machine running the bot. dusk supports local models through ollama or lm studio if that is the route you want.1

compare recurring costs on the right basis

as of 12 november 2026, dusk bot is free for personal use on your own computer, with every feature. ai usage is separate and billed by your ai provider. dusk cloud plus is $20/month or $200/year; pro is $50/month or $500/year. you are paying to keep the bots' computer running, not buying included ai usage.9

poke lists a free plan and pro at $19/month. manus lists a free plan and pro at $20/month with 4,000 monthly credits. these price points do not establish equal usage or equal capability.1011

ask whether your intended use fits the relevant limits before calling anything cheaper. dusk's free personal license does not cover work use. and if an ai plan runs out, a dusk bot says so; you wait for the reset or switch its ai. a hosting payment does not remove that limit.

where dusk fits and where to look elsewhere

dusk fits if you like texting distinct helpers and want to choose where they live. one bot can help with writing, another with a project, with separate jobs and memories. you can change a bot's model without losing its chats, files or memory.

if your main need is a cloud agent producing research or a presentation, compare manus on that job. if you enjoy mapping a process into a visual canvas and want to maintain it, compare n8n. those are differences in approach, not claims that either will finish your particular task without checking.

dusk is a weaker fit if you need a released phone-store app today or notifications from the current phone browser version. it also is not open source: it is source-available under polyform strict, with personal and work use treated differently.12

run a small personal trial

make one bot and attach a non-sensitive document you already understand. send: "help me prepare questions about this document. separate what it says from what you think i should ask. do not contact anyone or change a file."

check the questions against the document. give a correction, then try a second related task to see whether the ongoing conversation is useful. only add a routine when you know what repeated result you want.

keep the tool if you return to it because it helps. if the setup and checking are more work than the task, narrow the job or choose a simpler approach. you do not need a whole team to find out whether one helper fits.

questions

what are the best personal-life uses for an ai agent?

start with a task you can verify: preparing questions from a document, reviewing your own notes or repeating a narrow check. those are examples, not a ranking. the right choice depends on the context you can provide and the result you need.

can i get a personal ai agent for free?

dusk bot's local app is free for personal use. your ai provider may still charge, and cloud hosting is separate. competitor free plans have their own limits, so compare the job and allowance rather than the word free.

how are people running their agents from a phone?

with dusk, pair your phone to the machine where the bots live. the current phone version runs in the browser and sends no notifications. pairing to dusk cloud lets you text with your own computer off.

does a personal agent have to run in the cloud?

no. dusk bots can live on your computer, dusk cloud or your own server. local routines stop when that computer sleeps or the app closes. pick the location around when you need the work to happen.
