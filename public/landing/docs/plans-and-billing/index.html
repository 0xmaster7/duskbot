plans and billing

what is free, what plus and pro add, how yearly billing works, and how to pay, change or cancel.

dusk bot is free for personal use, with every feature. you pay only for a cloud computer or for a license to use it at work. current prices are always on the pricing page.

team plans start at $100 a month, with a dedicated machine and shared bots. write to hello@dusk.bot and we will set one up with you.

yearly billing

paying yearly gives you two months free: you pay for ten months and get twelve.

what a work license is

the app is free for personal use: study, hobby projects, your own life. using it at or for a business needs a license. pro includes one for one person, and a team plan covers a team.

the license page has the exact terms. if you are not sure which side of the line you are on, ask us.

what is never on your dusk bill

ai usage. your bots use the ai account you signed in with, and that provider bills you under your plan with them. a dusk plan pays for the cloud computer and the license.

paying

you pay by card at checkout. payments, tax and invoices are handled by dodo payments, our merchant of record. we store your plan and what you paid. we never see or store your card number.

invoices appear on the billing page of your account after your first payment.

changing or cancelling

the billing page of your account is where you change plan, switch between monthly and yearly, and cancel. you can cancel at any time.

two things people assume that are not so:

  • closing the app does not cancel a plan. neither does deleting a bot or disconnecting a workspace. cancel on the billing page.
  • cancelling dusk does not cancel your ai. your subscriptions with claude, chatgpt and the rest are separate, and stay as they are.

when a plan ends

your bots on your own computer or server carry on as before. your cloud computer is kept for a limited time and is then deleted, so move your bots back or export a backup first. the terms say how long.

questions about a charge

write to hello@dusk.bot from the email address on your account and we will sort it out.
