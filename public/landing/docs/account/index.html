docs/account and plans

your account

an account is optional. it shows your workspaces side by side and holds your plan.

you do not need an account to use dusk bot on your own computer or your own server. an account at app.dusk.bot is for two things: buying a plan, and seeing all your workspaces in one place.

sign in

go to app.dusk.bot and enter your email. we send a code to that address. type it in and you are signed in. there is no password to remember.

what the account shows

a workspace is one place your bots live: your mac, each server, your cloud. the account lists them side by side. for each one you can see:

  • its bots, with their names and which ai each one uses
  • how much work they did each day
  • the machine: processor, memory and disk
  • its bot computers, and whether each is awake
  • the phones and devices paired with it

from the same page you can do a few things from afar: wake or sleep a bot computer, pause or resume a routine, and remove a phone or a signed-in session.

connect a workspace

  1. in the desktop app, open the menu under your name and choose Account dashboard.
  2. your browser opens. sign in if you are not already.
  3. choose Connect.

the link is good for one use and for 30 minutes. to disconnect later, remove the workspace from the account. it stops reporting at once.

what a workspace tells the account

only counts and states. a workspace reports its machine's numbers, bot names, colours and models, the state of its bot computers, device names, and daily usage totals.

it never sends messages, prompts, files or keys. your chats stay where your bots live.

your plan

plans, invoices and your payment method are on the billing page of your account. see plans and billing.

download your data

the account page has Download your data. it gives you one json file with what the account service holds about you. it does not include your chats or files, because the account never had them. for those, make a backup in the app.

delete your account

the account page has Delete account. it removes the account, your sessions and your linked workspaces.

  • an active plan or a cloud computer has to be cancelled first. the page offers to do that.
  • deleting the account does not delete anything on your own computer or server.
  • it does not close your accounts with your ai providers. those are separate.

for anything else about your data, write to privacy@dusk.bot.
