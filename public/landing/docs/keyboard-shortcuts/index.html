keyboard shortcuts

every key the app listens for, on mac, windows and linux.

press ⌘/ on a mac, or Ctrl/ on windows and linux, to see this list inside the app. ? opens it too, when you are not typing.

getting around

what it doesmacwindows and linux
open the switcher: jump to a bot, or search your chats⌘KCtrlK
make a new bot⌘NCtrlN
jump to one of the first nine bots⌘1 to ⌘9Ctrl1 to Ctrl9
go to the previous or next bot⌘⇧[ or ⌘⇧]CtrlShift[ or CtrlShift]
hide or show the sidebar⌘\Ctrl\
find in this conversation⌘FCtrlF

chatting

what it doesmacwindows and linux
sendReturnEnter
new line without sending⇧ReturnShiftEnter
while a bot works: queue instead of steer, or the other way round⌘ReturnCtrlEnter
stop the bot you are looking at⌘.Ctrl.
stop every bot⌥⌘.CtrlAlt.
edit your last message, when the box is empty↑↑
close the open panel, dialog or find barEscEsc

you can change which key sends a message in settings. if you do, the list in the app shows your keys.

calls

what it doesmacwindows and linux
mute or unmute⌘⇧MCtrlShiftM
hang up⌘⇧HCtrlShiftH
interrupt the botSpaceSpace

groups and the sidebar

what it doesmacwindows and linux
save a group's instructions⌘ReturnCtrlEnter
move a sidebar section up or down, with its heading focused⌥↑ or ⌥↓Alt↑ or Alt↓
