relevance ai alternatives for a smaller job

relevance ai is aimed at companies building business agent teams, especially for sales and operations. dusk bot is a smaller starting point if you want to give a few bots jobs yourself. before comparing prices, note that relevance ai's own marketing page and docs disagree about available plans. so ask them which plan you can actually buy.

by the dusk bot team · checked 12 november 2026 · how we made this

the short version

pick relevance ai if

  • you are buying a business agent deployment and want to discuss it with a sales team
  • you need its documented native and pipedream connection catalogue
  • you want a platform offering unlimited workforces and curated multi-agent systems

pick dusk bot if

  • you want to define a bot by naming it and describing one job
  • you prefer separate bot memories and control over whether group conversations become memory
  • you want to try personal work on your own computer before choosing hosting

get a plan confirmed before budgeting

as of 12 november 2026, relevance ai's marketing pricing page shows enterprise with custom pricing and a request to talk to sales.2 its docs still list pro at $19/month billed annually or $29 monthly, and team at $234/month billed annually or $349 monthly. the docs say the free plan is retired for new and existing accounts.3

those are conflicting buying instructions from the same company. ask relevance ai which plans are available to a new account, which billing period applies and what is included in writing. we cannot tell you that the docs price will be offered at checkout.

the docs also list pro allowances of 2,500 actions and 10,000 vendor credits, plus extra usage at $80 per 1,000 actions and $20 per 10,000 vendor credits.3 ask about both meters, rather than treating credits as another name for completed jobs.

do you need a workforce or one careful reader?

relevance ai describes a platform for business workforces, with unlimited workforces and curated multi-agent systems.12 that is worth considering when deployment is an organisation-wide project. if you are still deciding what one agent should do, start with the smallest handoff you can review.

a dusk instruction to try is: "read these attached sales handoff notes. separate confirmed commitments from questions nobody has answered. keep the source filename with each item, and leave the originals alone." attaching files is supported.9

if you need several viewpoints, ai teammates explains that model of work. in dusk, a group can have one leader handing work to other bots. each retains its own ai and permissions. group chat learning is off unless you enable it for the bot.1011

keep the first deployment narrow

a founder trying a first assistant has a different buying problem from a company commissioning a sales workforce. list the documents, apps and decisions the first bot actually needs. do not connect every account because it is available.

dusk lets you choose app access per bot, including read-only access. sending messages or emails asks first unless you set a daily allowance. one exception: a bot on Full access with no sending rule of its own may send up to 25 a day without asking, so set Ask every time for it.12 other app changes are not documented to require approval, so keep the bot read-only or include "ask me before you change anything" in its instructions.9

if what you really want is a building platform with different billing, gumloop belongs in the comparison too. a simpler app isn't automatically safer or more accurate; we haven't measured either. check the current terms of any ai provider you sign in to as well.

how we checked

we make dusk bot, and this is a comparison of documented offerings, not an independent performance review. we read relevance ai's own pages on 12 november 2026. those pages disagree on plans, so we have not resolved the disagreement by guessing. we did not run identical tasks through both products or test a sales-led deployment.

questions

what is the current relevance ai price?

its own pages disagree as of 12 november 2026. marketing shows enterprise custom pricing; docs list pro and team prices. ask sales to confirm availability before using those docs figures in a budget.23

can non-technical business owners use an agent team?

start by writing one job and deciding how you will check it. relevance ai offers a deployment route through its sales team.2 dusk lets you make bots in an app and put them in group chats.610 neither approach makes unclear instructions clear by itself.

can i bring my own model credentials to relevance ai?

its plan docs say own api keys are optional on every plan, alongside vendor credits for model and tool costs. that is separate from which commercial plan a new buyer can get.4
