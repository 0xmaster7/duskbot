lindy ai alternatives for a smaller team of bots

lindy is worth keeping on your shortlist when you want a hosted employee across work tools, broad integrations and pooled team credits. dusk bot is a different fit: a small team of named bots, with an ai you choose for each and a separate provider bill. the comparison is not simply paid versus free. dusk's free desktop use is personal; work needs a license, and hosting costs extra.1234

by the dusk bot team · checked 12 november 2026 · how we made this

the short version

pick lindy if

  • you want a hosted work assistant reached through slack, email, imessage and calls
  • you value its listed integration breadth and a pool of credits shared across active users

pick dusk bot if

  • you want bots with distinct jobs and memory rather than one work assistant interface
  • you want to choose their ai providers and start with personal tasks on your own computer

the work plan behind the headline price

pricing is as of 12 november 2026. lindy's prices are per active user and depend on credits. dusk's free personal option is not a free work license.

why lindy can still be the better fit

lindy describes a work assistant available in places a team may already use, including slack and email. its pricing lists 3,000+ integrations, model selection and scheduled routines.12 if you need a particular business tool or delivery channel, verify that first. a lower bill is little help if the replacement cannot reach the tool your process needs.

dusk's documented list is 60+ connected apps and support for adding trusted mcp servers.9 check that your tools are on the list. the lindy versus dusk comparison is the direct next read; the ai employees guide helps you decide what kind of recurring work you actually want to hand over.

credits, active users and work licenses

as of 12 november 2026, lindy team starts at $29.99 per active user monthly for 3,000 credits. credits are pooled and do not roll over; top-ups cost $10 per 1,000 credits.2 ask how many people will be active and how much work shares that pool. the listed allowance does not tell us a fixed number of emails, calls or finished workflows.

dusk's personal desktop use is free, with ai billed by the provider. work requires a work license. cloud pro costs $50 monthly and includes one person's work license.348 account for the provider's bill as well as hosting before making a business budget.

try one job before designing a whole department

choose one bounded job that lets you inspect the result. for a dusk bot, attach the material it needs, give it a job in plain words, and read its work in the chat. files it makes appear there; local work lives in its folder or the project folder you give it.9

keep connected apps read-only during that trial. dusk asks before sends unless you allow a daily amount, but other connected-app changes are not documented as asking first. one exception: a bot on Full access with no sending rule of its own may send up to 25 a day without asking, so give full-access bots Ask every time under sending.11

for a small business, write down who checks the output and what happens when a run cannot finish. dusk's routines have run history and can be paused; that helps you inspect the job, not guarantee its correctness.10

how we checked

we make dusk bot. this comparison uses lindy's homepage and pricing page, checked on 12 november 2026, alongside dusk docs. we did not build identical workflows or measure task success. integration totals are each product's published count, not a count using one common definition. lindy's pricing page uses some inconsistent plan names, so we have not assigned computer-use features to a tier.

what do people actually use ai employees for?

is dusk bot cheaper than lindy for work?

lindy charges by active users and credits; dusk work use needs a license, ai usage has its own bill, and hosting is optional. free personal use is not the right price to compare with a team deployment.234
