a zapier agents alternative you talk to in its own app

zapier agents is worth keeping if your main need is an agent working across zapier's large app catalogue, on command or through triggers. dusk bot offers a desktop conversation with named bots, their own memory and a choice of local or hosted computers. the biggest budgeting trap is comparing zapier automation tasks with agents activities. they belong to separate plans, and neither tells you how many jobs you'll get done.

by the dusk bot team · checked 12 november 2026 · how we made this

the short version

pick zapier agents if

  • your required apps are in its 9,000+ app catalogue
  • you want cloud agents working from triggers without maintaining a computer
  • you want agent-to-agent calling inside the zapier system

pick dusk bot if

  • you want a desktop home for separate bot conversations and memories
  • you want to select a different ai for each bot and change it later
  • you want the bot to have a separate browser and desktop you can watch

agents activities are separate from zap tasks

zapier's pricing page separates agents from its automation plans. as of 12 november 2026, agents free includes 400 activities a month; agents pro lists 1,500 for $33.33/month billed annually ($400/year), and enterprise is custom.2

the automation free plan's 100 tasks and professional automation pricing belong to a different product allowance.2 do not substitute those numbers into an agents budget. zapier's pages we read don't say exactly which agent steps use an activity, so we don't estimate how many jobs 1,500 activities will finish.

ask zapier to show the activity accounting for your own proposed process. for another usage model, gumloop uses credits and orchestration fees, while n8n bills workflow executions.

check the action you need, not just the app name

zapier agents lists 9,000+ apps.1 dusk lists 60+ connected apps.6 the larger catalogue is a real reason to investigate zapier first when you have an unusual tool. it does not establish that every action you need is available or that every app works the same way.

before switching, write down the exact read, draft or change you need from each connection. this comparison has not checked those individual actions. avoid rebuilding a working process until you know its essential operation exists in the new tool.

for dusk, a bot can be limited to read-only app access or an exact list of actions. sends ask first unless you allow a daily amount. a bot on Full access with no sending rule of its own may send up to 25 a day without asking, so set Ask every time for it. the sending setting doesn't cover other app changes. to keep them supervised, give the bot read-only access or write "ask me before you change anything" into the job.611

make a handoff you can inspect

one low-stakes trial is a weekly file review: "every friday at 3, list the files you made in your folder this week. give me each filename and one line on what it contains. leave the files as they are." dusk bots keep the files they make in their own folders, along with their own chats and memory.46

if phone conversation is the reason you are looking elsewhere, compare the actual channel. dusk uses its own app and a paired phone browser today; its store apps are still on the way.5 the assistants you text comparison covers products available through messaging apps. when choosing a dusk bot's ai, check that provider's current terms for your intended use.

how we checked

this page compares zapier's agents and pricing pages, read on 12 november 2026, with dusk bot's docs. we make dusk bot. we did not run identical tasks, count the activities a real process would consume or test connector coverage app by app. catalogue size describes available connections, not identical actions or equal reliability in each product.

questions

how much does zapier agents cost?

as of 12 november 2026, free includes 400 monthly activities. pro is $400/year, displayed as $33.33/month billed annually, with 1,500 activities. enterprise pricing is custom. those are agents plans, separate from automation plans.2

will an agent run while my laptop is closed?

zapier describes agents running in its cloud on triggers and while you sleep.1 local dusk routines need your computer awake and the app open. dusk cloud is the hosted option when you need routines on time.89

is a zapier task the same as an agents activity?

the pricing page lists them as separate allowances.2 ask zapier for a worked example for your own process.
