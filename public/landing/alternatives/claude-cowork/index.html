claude cowork alternatives beyond the old platform gap

a claude cowork alternative should solve a need you still have today. windows support alone is no longer a reason to switch: claude covers macos, windows, linux beta, web and phones. as of 12 november 2026, cowork is being renamed to just claude. dusk bot's distinct choice is a small team whose ai can differ by bot, with local, own-server and hosted places to run.12347

by the dusk bot team · checked 12 november 2026 · how we made this

the short version

pick claude if

  • you want anthropic's own agent experience across folders, apps and connectors
  • you want hand-off and scheduled tasks within a paid claude plan, including phone-started work in the cloud

pick dusk bot if

  • you want one bot for each job and the option to give those bots different ai engines
  • you want to choose whether chats and memory live on your disk, your own server or a dusk cloud computer

check today's platforms before switching

the name and plan rollout are as of 12 november 2026. linux support is beta in both columns; it should not be read as a promise that every linux distribution is supported.

cowork is becoming part of claude

the current product banner says cowork is now just claude, rolling out to pro, max and ultra, with more plans to follow. file tasks can bring in those capabilities automatically.1 searching for cowork still makes sense, but the app may no longer use that name in the same place.

claude's free plan does not include hand-off and scheduled tasks in anthropic's pricing table. pro is $20 a month with monthly billing as of 12 november 2026.6 see the cowork pricing guide when the plan, rather than the product name, is your sticking point.

windows and linux deserve a current answer

claude already has a windows desktop app. its download page also lists a linux beta for ubuntu and debian with cowork available.2 an alternative page that treats it as mac-only is giving you an old reason to leave. the windows guide is the narrower starting point if that was your question.

dusk bot supports ubuntu 24.04 in beta through .deb or appimage, alongside macos and windows.3 do not infer support for every linux setup from that. on phones, dusk currently pairs through a browser version that sends no notifications. that is a meaningful limit beside claude's ios and android apps.52

engine choice is the clearer fork

claude is the direct route if you want anthropic's models in anthropic's app.6 dusk bot has no model of its own. it runs the ai vendors' own command-line apps, with your sign-in on the machine where your bots live.311 check your provider's current terms before choosing a sign-in; being listed in an app is not vendor endorsement.

you can switch a dusk bot's ai while it keeps its chats, files and memory.7 that helps if your jobs call for different engines, but it gives you another decision to manage. claude cowork versus dusk bot is useful if you want to weigh that flexibility against staying in one provider's product.

this is a documentation comparison by the people who make dusk bot. we did not run matching file tasks on every operating system or benchmark the engines. we checked anthropic's pages on 12 november 2026; the rename is a rollout notice, so the wording in your installed app may differ. the table compares documented availability, not equal quality on each platform.

questions

is there a claude cowork alternative for windows?

dusk bot supports windows 10 and 11, but claude itself now supports windows too. first check whether the current claude app meets your need before changing tools just for the operating system.23

is claude cowork free or paid?

the hand-off and scheduling features require a paid claude plan. as of 12 november 2026, pro is $20 monthly; free does not include those features. the rename to claude does not make them part of the free tier.16

where does my agent run when my laptop is closed?

since 6 october 2026, new cowork tasks on pro, max and ultra run in claude's cloud, and its scheduled tasks keep running while your computer sleeps.1213 a dusk bot on your own sleeping computer can't run routines; dusk cloud is the hosted option for that.49
