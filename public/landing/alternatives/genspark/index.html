genspark alternatives for people who want ongoing bots

genspark combines an ai workspace for documents and media with claw, an assistant you message from chat apps. dusk bot centres on a few ongoing bots, each with its own job, memory and ai choice. keep genspark if its creation tools are the reason you subscribed. consider dusk if you want that bot relationship with a separate ai-provider bill. claw has both cloud and local modes, so hosting alone is not the difference.1234

by the dusk bot team · checked 12 november 2026 · how we made this

the short version

pick genspark if

  • you want slides, sheets, docs and media tools inside an ai workspace
  • you want claw in existing chat apps, with a choice of its cloud computer or desktop local mode

pick dusk bot if

  • you want persistent bots you can name, brief and give different ai engines
  • you prefer choosing where bot memory lives and paying your ai provider separately from the host

separate the workspace from claw's computer

prices are as of 12 november 2026. genspark workspace credits and the claw cloud computer fee are separate parts of the offer; the cloud starting price below is promotional.

do you want a creation workspace or someone to keep briefing?

genspark's scope includes slides, spreadsheets, documents and media alongside agents.1 if you value those tools together, a bot app is not automatically a replacement. dusk bot doesn't have genspark's slide, sheet or media editors. manus alternatives is another useful comparison when your priority is finished deliverables.

dusk gives each bot one job, its own conversations and memory, and an ai you can change later.10 a useful first trial is to attach two planning notes and ask a bot to identify conflicting dates for you to review. that uses the chat and file relationship rather than assuming an equivalent workspace editor.9 ai teammates describes how those ongoing roles fit together.

claw has more than one place to live

claw can run on a dedicated always-on cloud computer or in local mode through genspark's macos and windows desktop app. local mode does not need a cloud computer subscription.1 that matters if you were leaving only to avoid a hosted machine.

the local-mode help warns about file-system access and suggests selecting a workspace folder.1 keep the files available to an agent deliberate. in dusk, start with the files you attach or a project folder you choose. for connected apps, use read-only permissions when the job is to inspect rather than change things.9 neither product's local option is a reason to give it every file.

price the recurring work as well as the computer

as of 12 november 2026, genspark plus starts at $24.99 monthly with 10,000 monthly credits; pro starts at $249.99 with 125,000. claw's cloud computer has a separate promotional starting fee of $9.99 monthly,12 and claw tasks consume shared credits.2 do not read that server rate as unlimited ai work.

dusk cloud similarly does not pay for your ai. its plus and pro prices cover the hosted computer; your ai provider bills usage.45 if a bot's ai allowance runs out, it tells you, and you can wait or change ai.10 the agent cost guide helps separate these costs. without measured credit use for your recurring job, neither column proves a saving.

how we checked

we make dusk bot. this page compares the documented genspark workspace and claw offer checked on 12 november 2026 with dusk bot's docs. we have not made matching slide decks, research reports or scheduled jobs in both products. model counts and credit allowances do not establish equal output quality. the promotional cloud rate is a dated offer, not a forecast of renewal pricing.

questions

is genspark claw only a cloud service?

no. its help centre also documents local mode in the desktop app, without a cloud computer subscription. the local option has file-system access, so choose the working folder carefully.1

are genspark claw scheduled tasks free?

claw's scheduled tasks consume credits. a free workspace allowance exists, but that is not a promise of unlimited recurring runs. the cloud computer also has its own fee if you choose that mode.12

is there a genspark alternative with ai teammates?

dusk bot offers separate bots and group chats where they can hand work over by mentions. it isn't a copy of genspark's workspace, though.11
