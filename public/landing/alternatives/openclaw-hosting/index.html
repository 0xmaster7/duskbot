openclaw hosting or a different cloud bot app?

managed openclaw hosting keeps openclaw and moves its server care to a host. dusk cloud hosts a different product: your dusk bots, chats, memory and routines. choose a managed openclaw offer if keeping openclaw is the requirement. choose dusk cloud if you want dusk's app and a computer we look after. compare renewal prices and ai charges separately; a promotional hosting rate is not the complete cost of an assistant.1234

by the dusk bot team · checked 12 november 2026 · how we made this

the short version

pick managed openclaw if

  • you already want openclaw's channels and skills, with a host handling server care
  • you prefer to pay for deployment and maintenance of the open-source project rather than move to another bot app

pick dusk bot if

  • you want named dusk bots and their own chats, with a dedicated hosted computer
  • you want to move existing dusk chats, memory and routines from your computer to dusk cloud

read the term as carefully as the rate

hostinger's $4.99 monthly figure is a promotional rate on a 24-month term, paid as $119.76 up front. its recorded renewal is $11.99 monthly. those are the terms shown as of 12 november 2026, and offers vary by region and date.1 elestio starts at $16 monthly with compute, storage, bandwidth, updates and backups included.2

dusk cloud plus is $20 monthly or $200 yearly; pro is $50 monthly or $500 yearly. pro includes a work license for one person.4 ai usage is not included in that price.5 use the always-on cost tool to think through hosting and ai as separate lines rather than comparing only the largest discount.

managed does not decide what the agent may do

hostinger and elestio both describe taking care of server security and backups.12 that is useful, but it does not answer which apps you should connect or which jobs you should delegate. openclaw supports skills and plugins, so choosing additions still deserves attention.6 the openclaw safety guide focuses on that question.

in dusk bot, give a new bot read-only connected-app access while checking its work. sending emails, messages, posts or payments asks first unless you allow a daily amount (a bot on Full access with no sending rule of its own may send up to 25 a day). other app changes are not documented as automatically asking; keep read-only access or write "ask me before you change anything" into the routine.7 hosting does not replace that boundary.

keep the product choice separate from the server choice

if openclaw already fits, managed hosting may solve the remaining problem without a product change. if only its installation put you off, openclaw without terminal setup explains its desktop installers too.8

dusk cloud is a route for dusk bots. its welcome moves their chats, memory and routines from your computer.4 that documented move is not evidence that an openclaw deployment can be imported. write down the jobs, instructions and schedules you need before choosing another app; do not assume the host can translate them.

with either kind of agent, you still own the decision to trust a result. this comparison cannot promise unattended success or that a host's backups cover every connected service.

how we checked

we make dusk bot and sell dusk cloud. this comparison uses the saved hostinger and elestio offers checked on 12 november 2026; maintenance descriptions are the hosts' claims, not an independent security assessment. we have not deployed identical jobs on these services, tested uptime or benchmarked their machines. the prices do not establish which service is cheapest for a completed task.

questions

is there an official hosted openclaw?

the openclaw project says it has no hosted tier. hostinger and elestio sell third-party managed offers. keep that distinction when comparing support and who you are paying.612

is openclaw hosting free?

the openclaw software is free under mit, but the managed offers here charge for hosting, and ai usage has its own arrangement. a free software license does not pay for the server or model calls.63

do i need to buy a mac mini to keep an agent running?

not for these hosted routes. they run on a host's servers; dusk cloud also keeps your bots available with your own computer off. a mac mini at home has its own purchase and power costs, which we don't compare here.124
