dusk bot/alternatives

an n8n alternative for people who do not want to build workflows

n8n fits when you want to build a workflow on a visual canvas and control its steps. dusk bot fits when you want to describe a job to a bot, review its work and steer it with another message. both can live on a server, so hosting is not the main difference. the choice is whether you want to maintain a process or keep giving a teammate instructions.

by the dusk bot team · checked 12 november 2026 · how we made this

the short version

pick n8n if

  • you want a visual canvas for multi-step workflows and agents
  • you want self-hosted community software under its sustainable use license
  • you prefer a price based on workflow executions rather than steps or users

pick dusk bot if

  • you want to create a routine by describing it in a chat
  • you want ongoing chats and memory attached to a bot with one job
  • you want an app on your computer before deciding whether to host the bots

first decide who should spell out the steps

n8n is a workflow platform with a visual canvas, including ai agents and multi-step processes.1 choose that shape if you want the process represented as steps you can inspect. a conversational bot is a different way to hand over responsibility: you describe the goal, then inspect what it did. neither approach removes the need to check the result.

for instance, try asking a dusk bot: "every friday at 4, list the files added to this project folder this week, with one line on each." routines are created in chat, and files can live in a project folder you give the bot.78 watch the first few runs before you trust it.

execution pricing is useful, but not the whole budget

as of 12 november 2026, n8n starter is 20 eur/month billed annually for 2,500 executions; pro is 50 eur/month billed annually for 10,000. business is 667 eur/month billed annually for 40,000 executions and is self-hosted only. community edition is free to self-host.2

a workflow execution is the billing unit, not each step or user. do not equate one execution with one dusk message or one ai task. they are different meters. n8n's cloud offering also includes ai model access and assistant credits.2 gumloop has another credit model worth comparing if that is your main concern.

a server does not turn one tool into the other

both products give you hosting choices. n8n offers its cloud and self-hosting; dusk offers your computer, your own server and dusk cloud.24 if you are comparing openclaw too, keep the interface question separate from the server question.

local dusk jobs stop when the app closes or the computer sleeps. a run missed by less than 12 hours runs once when you return; older ones are skipped.7 for a friday report that must arrive while you are away, choose where it will actually run before relying on scheduled tasks.

when connecting apps, start a new bot with read-only access. if you later allow changes, put "ask me before you change anything" in the routine. sending (emails, messages, posts) asks first unless you set a daily amount. a bot on Full access with no sending rule of its own may send up to 25 a day without asking, so set Ask every time for full-access bots. the sending setting doesn't cover other changes inside an app, so keep the bot read-only or write "ask me before you change anything" into the routine.810 your ai provider's current terms still decide what its sign-in allows.

how we checked

we read n8n's repository and pricing page on 12 november 2026 and used our own docs for dusk. we didn't build the same workflow in both.

questions

is there an n8n alternative for non-technical people?

dusk bot is worth trying if your aim is to give a bot a small job in plain words. it does not give you n8n's visual workflow canvas. write down whether you need to control the sequence or mainly review the final work before choosing.

where do your agents actually run when your laptop is closed?

n8n can run in its cloud or on your server.2 dusk bots can run on dusk cloud or your own server. the local dusk app cannot keep a job going while your computer sleeps.47

is n8n open source?

its repository calls it fair-code under the sustainable use license, with separate enterprise licensing. that is not osi open source. dusk bot is source-available too, with free personal use and a work license for work.16
