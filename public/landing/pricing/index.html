billing questions.

something else? write to hello@dusk.bot.

is the free plan really free?

yes, for personal use. the app runs on your computer or your own server with every feature. no card, no account and no trial timer.

what counts as work?

using dusk bot at or for a business. that needs a license: pro includes one for one person, and a team plan covers a team. study, hobby projects and personal use are free. the license has the exact words.

how does yearly billing work?

you pay for ten months and get twelve. plus is $200 a year and pro is $500 a year.

who bills me for the ai?

your ai provider, on the plan or key you already have. a dusk plan pays for the cloud computer and the license, not for ai usage.

do i need an account?

not for the free plan. an account at app.dusk.bot is only needed for a paid plan, and it is also where you see your workspaces side by side.

how do i pay, and where are my invoices?

by card at checkout. payments, tax and invoices are handled by dodo payments, our merchant of record. your invoices show up on the billing page of your account. we never see or store your card number.

can i cancel?

any time, from the billing page of your account. cancelling a dusk plan does not cancel your ai subscriptions. those are separate and stay yours.

what happens to my bots if i stop paying?

bots on your own computer or server are not affected. a cloud computer is kept for a limited time after the plan ends and is then deleted, so move or save what you need first. the terms say how long.
