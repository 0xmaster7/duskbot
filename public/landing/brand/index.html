brand

the mark, the bots, the colours and a few words, for when you write about dusk bot or put it next to your own thing.

the mark.

a chrome pointer with a face. it sits to the left of the name, on white or on near black.

the bots.

one flat shape, one solid colour, two eyes. ten shapes, and they are drawn in code, so they move. poke one.

the pictures.

bright pixel posters of calm places: sky, sea, hills, meadows. no computers and no people in them. the bots are never drawn into the picture, they stand on top of it.

a few words.

if you need a short description, this one is right.

Dusk Bot is a desktop app for macOS, Windows and Linux where you run a team of AI bots and message them like teammates. Each bot has a job, a memory and a computer of its own, and runs on the AI account you already have. It is free for personal use.

do and don't

do

  • write the name as two words: Dusk Bot. lowercase is fine when everything around it is lowercase.
  • leave the mark some room, on white or near black.
  • show the bots flat, in one solid colour, with their two eyes.
  • use real pictures of the app when you show the app.

don't

  • redraw, recolour, stretch or tilt the mark.
  • give a bot texture, outlines, arms, or a mouth it did not come with.
  • put the mark on a busy picture.
  • suggest we made, sponsor or endorse something we did not.
