dusk bot/use cases

an ai assistant for the admin around your business

start with the admin that interrupts the actual business: customer questions, invoice threads and tomorrow's bookings. a dusk bot helper can read the mail and calendar you allow, organise what needs a decision and draft replies for you. you can also attach reviews for reply drafts. business use needs a work license; keep the first routines read-only and check their results yourself.

by the dusk bot team · checked 12 november 2026 · how we made this

a helper around opening hours

whenwhat a bot does
before openingsee customer messages needing an answer before today's appointments
mid-morningread an invoice query alongside the invoice you attached, with any amount disagreement made visible
between appointmentsask for a polite response to a review you pasted, without inventing what happened
before closingcheck tomorrow's bookings for overlaps or missing details, leaving the calendar alone
weekly admin timeuse the receipts sweep to compare emailed receipts with the spending sheet you maintain

the routines

the opening inbox list

every working day at 8:30, read my business gmail since yesterday's closing time. put today's customer questions first, then supplier questions and anything with an explicit deadline. draft suggested answers here using my attached hours and policies. ask about gaps. don't send, archive, label or delete mail.

set your working days and closing time first; email triage has a fuller inbox setup

the invoice question check

every monday at 10, find unanswered invoice questions in my business inbox. compare each with the invoice files and payment notes i supplied. list invoice reference, the customer's question and any conflicting amount or date. draft a neutral reply in chat. don't assume silence means an invoice is unpaid, and don't contact anyone.

this uses the records you give it, not a bank connection; see invoice follow-up for the related workflow

the next-day booking check

each afternoon at 4:30, read tomorrow's bookings in my business google calendar. flag overlaps and bookings missing a service, location or contact detail. draft any clarification questions here, without sending them. don't move, cancel or create bookings. if the diary is clear, give me just the opening appointment time.

calendar read access is sufficient; add only the calendar that actually holds confirmed bookings

give it the rules customers ask about

attach your opening hours, service list and current cancellation policy. tell the bot where these came from and which version to use. when a customer asks for an exception, the bot should bring you the request rather than invent a rule.

start with drafts in the dusk bot chat. that lets you keep gmail read-only while you decide whether the answers sound like your business. later, you can allow specific draft actions if saving replies in the mailbox would help.

reviews need the facts of the visit

paste a review or attach it and ask for a short response. give the bot only the visit details needed for that reply. tell it not to invent a resolution, claim that you called someone or reveal a customer's private information.

here, you paste the review in and post the reply yourself. if the complaint needs an investigation, a draft acknowledging that is more useful than a made-up explanation.

budget for work use and check the diary yourself

using dusk bot for your business needs a work license. dusk cloud pro includes one for one person; see pricing for the plans. ai usage is billed separately by your ai provider. the free personal-use terms do not cover business admin.

these routines need your computer awake and the app running, or another place for the bots to live such as dusk cloud. keep your normal booking checks in place while trying the helper. it only sees the calendar and mail you connected, not every booking or payment made elsewhere.

questions

can a non-technical business owner set up the routines?

yes. make a bot with New bot, connect the supported apps and send a routine as a message. begin with one calendar or inbox and a small request you can check.

is it free to use for my business?

no. free use is personal use on your own computer. business work needs a work license, and ai usage remains on your provider's bill.

can it chase every unpaid invoice?

not from email alone. give it current invoice and payment records, then review any proposed follow-up. a missing receipt in an inbox is not enough evidence to tell a customer they owe money.
