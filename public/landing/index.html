what dusk bot is

dusk bot is a desktop app where you run a team of ai bots and text them like teammates. each one has a name, a job, a memory and, if you want, its own computer .

give each bot a job.

one bot, one job, one chat. start with one and hire as you go.

  • nova, researchreads the 40 tabs so you don't. comes back with sources, a table and a straight answer.
  • juno, inboxreads what came in overnight, drafts replies in your voice, leaves the three that need you on top.
  • rivet, codepicks up an issue, opens a pull request, and fixes what the review finds.
  • tally, bookkeepingmatches receipts to charges, and tells you when a subscription quietly got pricier.
  • quill, travelwatches fares, holds the good one, and checks you in the day before.
  • echo, socialsdrafts the week's posts from what you shipped. posts nothing until you say so.

work with many bots at once.

start with one. add another when the work grows. each has its own chat, job and memory, and they all work at the same time.

each bot gets its own computer

a browser and a desktop it signs in to and clicks through. watch it live, and take the mouse back whenever you like.

ask once and it becomes a routine

it runs on a schedule, or the moment something happens. routines run while you sleep.

it remembers how you like things

each bot keeps notes on what you like and who's who. every note is yours to read and edit.

put bots in a room

several bots in one thread hand work to each other. you step in when one of them needs a yes.

also: voice, so a bot can talk back and you can call it. and 60+ apps plus any mcp server you add.

is dusk bot really free?

yes, for personal use. the app runs on your computer with every feature. you only pay for a work license or a cloud computer.

which ai does it use?

the ones you already have. sign in with claude, chatgpt, gemini, grok and others, or add an api key. replies go straight from your machine to them, on your plan.

where does my data go?

nowhere, unless you choose a cloud computer. bots, chats, files and memory live on your machine or your own server.

what is a cloud computer?

a desktop we host for your bots, so routines keep running when your laptop is closed. each bot can have its own screen to work on, and you can watch it.

can i run it on my own server?

yes. open the app, choose add a server, and paste the line it shows. that is free too.

how is this different from a chatbot?

a chatbot answers. a bot here has a computer, signs in to your tools, runs on a schedule and remembers how you like things done.

is it safe to let bots click around?

bots ask before the scary stuff: sending, buying, deleting. you can watch or take over at any time.
