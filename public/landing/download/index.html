Download Dusk Bot

Signed builds for macOS, Windows and Linux. The app tells you when a new version is out; you choose when to install it.

Downloads open with the first public release. Until then, write to hello@dusk.bot for early access.

.deb AppImage

Check what you downloaded

Every release lists the SHA-256 of each installer in SHA256SUMS beside the files, signed into the update manifest at /releases/latest.json. On a Mac: shasum -a 256 DuskBot-*.dmg.

Run it on a server

To keep your bots on a VPS of your own, open the app, choose Add a server, and paste the line it shows on the server. See Docs.
