is dusk bot really free?

yes, for personal use. the app runs on your computer with every feature and no trial timer. you only pay for a cloud computer or a work license.

which ai does it use?

the one you already have. sign in with claude, chatgpt, gemini or grok, or use cursor, copilot, deepseek, kimi, qwen, opencode, cline or your own api key. dusk bot has no model of its own.

so who bills me for the ai?

your ai provider, exactly as it does today. dusk adds no second bill and no markup.

where does my data go?

nowhere, unless you pick a cloud computer. bots, chats, files and memory live on your disk or your own server.

what is a cloud computer?

a computer we host for your bots, so routines keep running with your laptop shut. it sleeps when idle and wakes itself for work.

can i run it on my own server?

yes, and that is free too. paste one line on a server you already have and your bots stay on all day.

is it safe to let a bot click around?

bots ask before the scary stuff: sending, buying, deleting. you can watch a bot's screen live and take the mouse back at any moment.

can i change the model later?

any time, and per bot. the bot keeps its chats, files and memory when you swap.
