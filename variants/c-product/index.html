is dusk bot really free?

yes, for personal use. you get every feature, on your own computer or your own server, with no trial timer. you pay only for a cloud computer or a work license.

which ai does it use?

the one you already pay for. dusk bot has no model of its own: you sign in with claude, chatgpt, gemini or grok. cursor, copilot, deepseek, kimi, qwen, opencode, cline and your own api key work too. each bot can use a different one, and when you swap, the bot keeps its chats, files and memory.

where does my data go?

nowhere, unless you choose a cloud computer. on your computer or your own server, bots, chats, files and memory stay on your disk.

what is a cloud computer?

a computer we host for your bots, so routines keep running with your laptop shut. it sleeps when idle and wakes for work.

is it safe to let bots click around?

bots ask before the scary stuff: sending, buying, deleting. you allow or deny. you can watch a bot's computer live and take the mouse back at any time.

what can a bot connect to?

more than 60 apps: gmail, google calendar, drive, docs and sheets, slack, notion, github, linear, figma, stripe, discord, hubspot, airtable, asana, trello, dropbox, outlook, shopify, sentry, vercel, supabase, posthog, zapier and more. you can also add any mcp server yourself.

can a bot talk?

yes. a bot can talk back, and you can call it and talk the job through.

can i use it for work?

yes, with a license. one comes with pro, for one person, and with team.
