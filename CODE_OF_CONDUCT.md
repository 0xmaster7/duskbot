# Code of conduct

dusk bot follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and constructive, assume good faith, and welcome people of every background and experience
level. Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language and publishing others' private information are not
acceptable, in this repository or in any dusk bot space.

Report unacceptable behaviour to **hello@dusk.bot**. Reports are handled privately, and maintainers may remove content
or block anyone who breaks this code.
