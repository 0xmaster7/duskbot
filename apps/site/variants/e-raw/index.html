what dusk bot is

dusk bot is a desktop app where you run a team of ai bots and text them like teammates. each one has a name, a job, a memory and, if you want, its own computer .

give each bot a job.

these six are samples. make your own: name it, pick a shape, and tell it the job in plain words.

  • nova

    researchon claude

    reads the forty tabs so you don't. comes back with a table and a straight answer.

  • juno

    inboxon chatgpt

    sorts what came in overnight and drafts the replies in your voice. you press send.

  • rivet

    codeon cursor

    picks up the issue, opens the pull request, fixes what the review finds.

  • tally

    bookkeepingon gemini

    matches receipts to charges and tells you when a subscription quietly got pricier.

  • quill

    travelon grok

    watches the fares, holds the good one, and asks before it books anything.

  • echo

    socialson claude

is it really free?

yes. for personal use you get every feature, forever, with no account. you only pay if you want a cloud computer or a license to use it at work.

then how do you make money?

from the cloud computers and the work licenses. that is the whole business model. there is no markup on ai, because we never touch that bill.

which ai does it use?

the one you already pay for: claude, chatgpt, gemini or grok. also cursor, copilot, deepseek, kimi, qwen, opencode, cline, or your own api key. dusk bot has no model of its own.

where does my stuff go?

on your computer, nowhere: it all stays on your disk. on your own server, your server. on a dusk cloud computer, that computer, which we host for you.

is it safe to let a bot click around?

bots ask before the scary stuff: sending, buying, deleting. you allow or deny. you can also watch any bot's screen live and take the mouse back.

what happens when i close my laptop?

bots on your computer stop until you are back. bots on a server or a dusk cloud computer keep going. the cloud one sleeps when idle and wakes up for work.

can i switch models later?

any time, one bot at a time. the bot keeps its chats, its files and its memory.

can i use it at work?

yes, with a license. pro includes one for one person, and team plans start at $100 a month.
