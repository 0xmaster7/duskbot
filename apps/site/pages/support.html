the phone app

the phone app is a remote for the bots on your own computer or server. it does not run bots by itself.

  • pairingopen dusk bot on your computer, choose your phone, and scan the code with the phone app. no account is needed for your own computer.
  • no repliescheck the computer is on and dusk bot is open there. replies arrive once it is back.
  • notificationsallow them when the app asks, or later in your phone's settings for dusk bot.
  • a reply that is wrong or harmfulopen the message's menu and tap report this reply, or write to hello@dusk.bot.
