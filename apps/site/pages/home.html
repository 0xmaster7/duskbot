is dusk bot really free?{{i plus}}

yes, for personal use. the app runs on your computer with every feature. you only pay for dusk cloud or a work license.

what is dusk cloud?{{i plus}}

a computer we host for your bots. your bots keep working when your laptop is off: routines run on time, day and night. nothing to set up, and each bot can have its own screen that you can watch.

which ai does it use?{{i plus}}

the ones you already have. sign in with claude, chatgpt, gemini, grok and others, or add an api key. replies go straight from your machine to them, on your plan.

which apps can a bot use?{{i plus}}

60+ to start: gmail, google calendar, slack, notion, github, linear, figma, stripe and more. you connect an app once and pick which bots may use it. you can also add any mcp server yourself.

where does my data go?{{i plus}}

on your computer, nowhere: bots, chats, files and memory stay on your disk. on dusk cloud they live on your own private cloud computer, and only you and your bots use it.

how is this different from a chatbot?{{i plus}}

a chatbot answers. a bot here has a computer, signs in to your tools, runs on a schedule and remembers how you like things done.

is it safe to let bots click around?{{i plus}}

bots ask before the scary stuff: sending, buying, deleting. you can watch or take over at any time.

can i run it on my own server?{{i plus}}

yes, and it is free. this is for people who look after their own servers. the own server guide has the one line to paste.
