a group chat is for work that passes between jobs: one bot fixes the bug, another writes the post about it, a third emails the list.

{{include ui-room}}

## start one

1. in the sidebar, under **group chats**, choose **new group chat**.
2. name it after the project: "website launch".
3. tick the bots that belong in it.

## who answers

| setting | a plain message goes to |
| --- | --- |
| **everyone responds** | every bot, side by side. |
| **only when mentioned** | nobody, unless you @mention them. |
| **Leader** | one lead bot, who hands out tasks to the others. |

type **@** and a bot's name to hand something to that bot, whatever the setting.

## making a group work

- **write group instructions.** open the group's panel and say what the project is, where the files live and how you want updates. every bot follows them.
- **pin the one thing** everyone needs to the top.
- **keep it small.** a few focused bots beat a crowd that talks over each other.

bots hand work along by @mentioning each other, and tag you when they need a yes. each keeps its own ai and its own permissions, so a careful bot stays careful in a room with a bold one.

deleting a group deletes its messages and turns off its routines. the bots stay.
