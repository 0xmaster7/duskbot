a few words.

if you need a short description, this one is right.

Dusk Bot is a desktop app for macOS, Windows and Linux where you run a team of AI bots and message them like teammates. Each bot has a job, a memory and a computer of its own, and runs on the AI account you already have. It is free for personal use.

do and don't

do

  • {{i check}}write the name as two words: Dusk Bot. lowercase is fine when everything around it is lowercase.
  • {{i check}}leave the mark some room, on white or near black.
  • {{i check}}show the bots flat, in one solid colour, with their two eyes.
  • {{i check}}use real pictures of the app when you show the app.

don't

  • {{i x}}redraw, recolour, stretch or tilt the mark.
  • {{i x}}give a bot texture, outlines, arms, or a mouth it did not come with.
  • {{i x}}put the mark on a busy picture.
  • {{i x}}suggest we made, sponsor or endorse something we did not.
