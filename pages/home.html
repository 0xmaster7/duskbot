is dusk bot really free?

yes, for personal use. the app runs on your computer with every feature. you only pay for a work license or a cloud computer.

which ai does it use?

the ones you already have. sign in with claude, chatgpt, gemini, grok and others, or add an api key. replies go straight from your machine to them, on your plan.

where does my data go?

nowhere, unless you choose a cloud computer. bots, chats, files and memory live on your machine or your own server.

what is a cloud computer?

a desktop we host for your bots, so routines keep running when your laptop is closed. each bot can have its own screen to work on, and you can watch it.

can i run it on my own server?

yes. open the app, choose add a server, and paste the line it shows. that is free too.

how is this different from a chatbot?

a chatbot answers. a bot here has a computer, signs in to your tools, runs on a schedule and remembers how you like things done.

is it safe to let bots click around?

bots ask before the scary stuff: sending, buying, deleting. you can watch or take over at any time.
