Get started

1. Install the app

Download it from dusk.bot/download and open it. No account is needed to use it on your own computer.

2. Connect an AI

Dusk Bot does not ship a model of its own. Your bots run on an AI you already use: Claude, ChatGPT (Codex), Gemini, Grok and others, signed in with your own account, or an API key you add in Settings. The first screen shows which ones are on this computer and how to sign in to each.

3. Make a bot

Choose New bot, give it a job, and start a chat. Bots can use their own browser and computer, and ask before they do anything you have not allowed.

Your own server

Keep your bots running on a VPS you rent (Ubuntu 22.04 or 24.04, 2 GB of memory or more). In the app choose Add a server; it shows one line with a one-time code. Paste it on the server as root or a sudo user:

curl -fsSL https://dusk.bot/install.sh | sh -s <code>

The installer is the same file as https://link.dusk.bot/i/<code>, which the app may show instead. It only talks to https addresses, checks the SHA-256 of everything it downloads, and is safe to run again. To read it before running it:

curl -fsSLo install.sh https://dusk.bot/install.sh
curl -fsSL https://dusk.bot/install.sh.sha256   # compare with: shasum -a 256 install.sh
less install.sh
sh install.sh <code>

To remove it again: curl -fsSL https://link.dusk.bot/uninstall | sh.

Your account

An account at app.dusk.bot is optional. It shows your workspaces side by side and holds plans and cloud desktops. You can download your data or delete the account from its Account page.
