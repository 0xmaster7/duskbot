your bots have to run somewhere. there are three places, and the app works the same in all of them. you can start in one and move later.

{{include ui-where}}

## the three places

| | on your computer | on dusk cloud | on your own server |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **cost** | free | plus or pro | free, plus what the server costs you |
| **on when your laptop is shut** | no | yes | yes |
| **where your things are stored** | your disk | your cloud computer | your server |
| **who looks after it** | you | we do | you |
| **needs an account** | no | yes | no |

### on your computer

this is where everyone starts. bots, chats, files and memory stay on your disk. there is no account and nothing to rent.

the catch is simple: when your computer sleeps, your bots stop. [routines](/docs/routines) that were due while it slept run late or are skipped.

### on dusk cloud

a computer we host for your bots. routines keep running with your laptop shut. it sleeps when idle and wakes for work. it comes with the plus and pro [plans](/pricing).

read more in [dusk cloud](/docs/dusk-cloud).

### on your own server

a server you rent and look after yourself. your bots stay on as long as it does. you paste one line and the app sets everything up.

read more in [your own server](/docs/your-own-server).

## how to choose

- **just trying it, or you only need bots while you work:** your computer.
- **you want routines to run overnight and do not want to run a server:** dusk cloud.
- **you already rent a server, or want everything on a machine you control:** your own server.

## switching between them

the menu at the top of the sidebar is where you switch between **This computer** and your cloud or server. it is also where you add one.

you can keep more than one. some people keep a few bots on their laptop and the always-on ones on a cloud.

## moving your bots

when you add a cloud or a server, the app offers to move this computer's bots there, with their chats, memory and routines. after the move they work from the new place, and this computer becomes a window onto it.

to move everything by hand, or to keep a copy, use a [backup](/docs/privacy-and-data#backups).

## your ai comes along

wherever your bots live, they use your own ai account. on a cloud or a server you sign in to your ai there once, from your own computer. see [connecting an ai](/docs/connect-an-ai#on-a-server-or-a-cloud-computer).

## your phone works with all three

the [phone app](/docs/phone) pairs with whichever place your bots live. paired with a cloud or a server, it keeps working when your computer is off.
