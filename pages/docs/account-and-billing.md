dusk bot is free for personal use, with every feature, on your own computer. you need an account only to buy a plan.

## plans

| | free | plus | pro |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **price** | $0, forever | $20 a month | $50 a month |
| **yearly** | | $200 a year | $500 a year |
| **every feature of the app** | yes | yes | yes |
| **[dusk cloud](/docs/dusk-cloud) computer** | no | 2 vcpu, 4 gb, 40 gb | 4 vcpu, 8 gb, 80 gb |
| **work license** | add one, $12 a month | add one, $12 a month | yes, for one person |

yearly is two months free. a **team** license is $30 a month for 2 people and $18 for each extra person, up to 50; add it yourself on the billing page. a team plan with a dedicated dusk cloud (8 vcpu, 16 gb, 160 gb) is $90 a month: write to hello@dusk.bot to set that one up. current prices are on [the pricing page](/pricing).

**ai usage is never on your dusk bill.** your ai provider bills you on your plan with them.

using dusk bot at or for a business needs a work license. pro includes one for one person, or you can add one on its own for $12 a month (on free or plus) from the billing page of your account. the [license page](/license) has the exact terms.

## your account

go to [dusk.bot/dashboard]({{app}}) and enter your email. we send a code; there's no password. the account shows your plan, invoices, and each place your bots live, with their bots, usage and bot computers. it never sees your messages, prompts, files or keys.

payments, tax and invoices are handled by dodo payments, our merchant of record. we never see your card number.

## changing or cancelling

change plan, switch to yearly, or cancel any time from the billing page of your account.

- **closing the app doesn't cancel a plan.** cancel on the billing page.
- **cancelling dusk doesn't cancel your ai.** claude, chatgpt and the rest are separate.
- **when a plan ends,** bots on your own computer carry on. a cloud computer is kept for 30 days, then deleted (we email you a week before).

questions about a charge? write to hello@dusk.bot from the email on your account.

## your data and deleting the account

the account page has **Download your data** and **delete account**. deleting it doesn't touch anything on your own computer or server, and an active plan has to be cancelled first (the page offers to). a cloud computer on the account stops (at once, or when its paid time ends), is kept for 30 days, then deleted.
