press [[⌘ /]] on a mac, or [[Ctrl /]] on windows and linux, to see this list inside the app. [[?]] opens it too, when you are not typing.

## getting around

| what it does | mac | windows and linux |
| --- | --- | --- |
| open the switcher: jump to a bot, or search your chats | [[⌘ K]] | [[Ctrl K]] |
| make a new bot | [[⌘ N]] | [[Ctrl N]] |
| jump to one of the first nine bots | [[⌘ 1]] to [[⌘ 9]] | [[Ctrl 1]] to [[Ctrl 9]] |
| go to the previous or next bot | [[⌘ ⇧ []] or [[⌘ ⇧ ]]] | [[Ctrl Shift []] or [[Ctrl Shift ]]] |
| hide or show the sidebar | [[⌘ \]] | [[Ctrl \]] |
| find in this conversation | [[⌘ F]] | [[Ctrl F]] |

## chatting

| what it does | mac | windows and linux |
| --- | --- | --- |
| send | [[Return]] | [[Enter]] |
| new line without sending | [[⇧ Return]] | [[Shift Enter]] |
| while a bot works: queue instead of steer, or the other way round | [[⌘ Return]] | [[Ctrl Enter]] |
| stop the bot you are looking at | [[⌘ .]] | [[Ctrl .]] |
| stop every bot | [[⌥ ⌘ .]] | [[Ctrl Alt .]] |
| edit your last message, when the box is empty | [[↑]] | [[↑]] |
| close the open panel, dialog or find bar | [[Esc]] | [[Esc]] |

you can change which key sends a message in settings. if you do, the list in the app shows your keys.

| what it does | mac | windows and linux |
