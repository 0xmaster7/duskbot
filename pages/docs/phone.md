your bots keep running where they live. your phone is a window onto them: send messages, watch replies arrive, stop a bot, and tap **Allow** on an approval without walking back to your desk.

## pair your phone

you pair once, by scanning a code. no account needed.

1. on your computer, open the menu under your name and choose **Connect your phone**.
2. choose **to this computer**, or **to the Cloud** if you have [dusk cloud](/docs/dusk-cloud).
3. scan the code with your phone's camera and confirm the computer's name.

{{include ui-pair}}

can't scan? the same screen offers a six-digit code, a nearby list on your wi-fi, or tailscale.

> note: the phone app is on its way to the app store and google play. until then, the code opens dusk bot in your phone's browser. add it to your home screen (safari: share, **Add to Home Screen**; chrome: **Install app**) and it opens like any other app. the home screen version doesn't send notifications.

## your computer has to be awake

paired with your computer, the phone only works while that computer is on and running dusk bot. **Settings**, **Remote access**, **Keep this computer awake** helps. pair with [dusk cloud](/docs/dusk-cloud) and it works with your computer off.

## notifications

the store app tells you when a reply, a question or an approval arrives. a notification shows the bot's name and "new message", never the text.

## paired devices

**Settings**, **Remote access** lists every paired device. remove one and it loses access at once.
