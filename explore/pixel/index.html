the pixel crowd

the best twenty, picked to read at sidebar size. one colour per bot, pure white eyes, nothing else: no extras around them, every mood is the body and the eyes. at rest they stay still: they blink, their eyes shift a pixel toward a near pointer, and every so often one settles by a pixel. a busy bot moves with its whole body (it leans in, types, hops, turns, droops) in loops with pauses, and a tap is a bouncy reaction that settles in under a second.

    small sizes

    the same drawings re-rasterised at the size they are shown, not shrunk. 16, 32 and 48 use the 16 grid at 1x, 2x and 3x; 24 is drawn on a 24 grid at 1x so the eyes keep room inside the body. busy chats move at sidebar size too, by whole pixels.

      on dark

      secondary check. every pixel is either the body colour or the white eyes, so there is no fringe on any background.

      the engine

      plain javascript, no dependencies, one file. bodies are small shapes re-rasterised to the pixel grid whenever they squash, lean or twitch an ear, so motion stays pixel-true at any size. springs run underneath and only whole pixels come out. at rest a bot is still and the loop sleeps between blinks; busy states run on beats with pauses so a loop stays restful; eyes never leave the body, with at least one body pixel around them. one shared loop that stops when nothing is on screen; reduced motion keeps the eye changes and drops the movement.
